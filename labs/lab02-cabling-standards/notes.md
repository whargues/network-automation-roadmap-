# Lab 02 – Cabling & Standards

## Objective
Understand Ethernet standards, cabling types, and fiber optics. Practice connecting different equipment in Packet Tracer using the correct cables.

## Skills Practiced
- Identified IEEE Ethernet standards:
  - 10BASE‑T / 100BASE‑T → 2 pairs (transmit/receive)
  - 1000BASE‑T / 10GBASE‑T → 4 pairs
- Learned Auto‑MDIX feature → modern devices auto‑detect crossover vs straight‑through
- Differentiated fiber types:
  - MMF (multi‑mode fiber) for shorter distances
  - SMF (single‑mode fiber) for longer distances
- Reviewed SFP modules and their standards/ranges
- Practiced cabling in Packet Tracer:
  - Straight‑through vs crossover cables
  - SMF vs MMF depending on distance
- Connected routers, switches, PCs, and servers with correct cable types

## Reflection
This lab focused on physical standards and cabling rather than configs.  
I now understand the differences between Ethernet speeds, copper vs fiber, and how modern equipment simplifies cabling with Auto‑MDIX.  
Practicing connections in Packet Tracer reinforced how to choose the right cable type for each scenario.
